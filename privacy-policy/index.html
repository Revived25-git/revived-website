Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Revived Detail (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through reviveddetail.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request a quote we collect the details you submit: your name, phone number, email, address or area, vehicle information and any notes. Our hosting provider and basic site tools may also record standard technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and perform service, send appointment reminders and follow-ups, and improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example our scheduling and messaging software) and when required by law.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your quote or appointment. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Your choices

To ask us to update or delete your information, email thereviveddetail@gmail.com or call 918-200-9554.
