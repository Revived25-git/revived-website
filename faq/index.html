FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you need to know before booking your first detail.

What is Revived Detail?

Revived is a mobile auto detailing business based in Tulsa, Oklahoma. We come to your home, apartment or office to provide professional interior and exterior detailing, so there is no shop visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the quote form on our Contact page, or call or text us at 918-200-9554. Tell us where you are and what your car needs and we will follow up with next steps.

Where do you detail the car? Can you come to apartments or parking garages?

We detail wherever your car is parked: driveways, apartment complexes, office lots and parking garages. If you are in a gated community or garage, let us know the access details when you book.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. We bring our own water and power.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Yes. Revived is licensed and insured, and we stand behind every detail we do.

What if I am not satisfied with the results?

We are human and accidents happen. If anything does not meet your expectations, let us know and we will come back and fix it, free of charge.

Do I need a service plan, or can I book one time?

Service plans are optional. You can book a one-time interior or full detail any time, but Revived Care Plan members save $60 to $100 per visit with Quarterly, Bi-Monthly or Monthly scheduling.

How much is the exterior detail?

The exterior is $50 when added to an interior detail.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Tulsa and serve the greater Tulsa metro, including Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Owasso, Sand Springs, Sapulpa, Glenpool, Coweta, Catoosa, Claremore, Collinsville and Skiatook.

Do you detail fleets and company vehicles?

Yes. Businesses across the area count on Revived for fleet and commercial detailing. Ask for a commercial quote.

Still have a question?

Call or text 918-200-9554 or request a quote.
