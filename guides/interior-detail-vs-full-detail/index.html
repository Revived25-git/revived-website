Interior detail vs. full detail: what is the difference?

Two services, one simple difference, and how to pick the right one.

Detailing menus can be confusing. Here is how ours works.

Interior detail

The interior detail cleans the cabin: trash removal, a compressor blowout of vents and seams, a full vacuum, disinfected console, dash and cup holders, wiped seats, scrubbed floor mats, cleaned doors and streak-free windows, plus light stain and pet hair removal.

Full detail

The full detail is the interior detail plus the exterior: a hand wash with foam pre-soak, wheels, tires and wheel wells cleaned, tire dressing, bugs and road grime removed, door jambs and trim cleaned, towel-dried glass, and a 1-month spray wax.

How to choose

  • Choose interior if the paint looks fine and the cabin is where you feel the mess.
  • Choose full detail if you are selling, preparing for an event, or have not detailed in a while. The exterior is only $50 on top of the interior, so most customers go full.
  • Add steam and shampoo for stained seats and carpets, or odor removal for smells.
