How to get bad smells out of your car

Air fresheners just mask the problem. Here is how to actually fix it.

Every car smell has a source. Find it and the fix gets easy.

Step 1: Find the source

Check under seats, in seat folds, in the trunk, in door pockets and under the floor mats. Old food, a spilled drink or a damp mat are common culprits.

Step 2: Remove the cause

  • Throw out trash and old food.
  • Take floor mats out and dry or clean them.
  • Vacuum thoroughly.

Step 3: Clean the fabric

Odors live in fabric. Hot-water extraction (steam and shampoo) clears them out better than a surface wipe.

Step 4: Check the cabin air filter and vents

A dirty cabin filter can make the whole car smell stale.

When it is more than a spill

Mold, vomit and heavy smoke or pet odors usually need a professional approach. We offer odor removal and a Bio-Detail add-on for the worst jobs.
