How to prepare for a mobile detail appointment

Five quick things that help your detail go smoothly.

  1. Clear out personal items. Take valuables and loose belongings out of the car so we can reach every surface.
  2. Tell us about access. Gate codes, garage codes, parking garage details and apartment instructions help us arrive on time.
  3. Mention problem spots. Stains, odors, pet hair or a specific mess help us plan the right time and services.
  4. Leave the car reachable. You do not need to be there, but we need to be able to get to the vehicle.
  5. Pick a spot with room. A driveway, office lot or parking space works. We bring our own water and power.

That is it. Request a quote and we will handle the rest.
