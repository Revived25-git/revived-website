Meet the Team

Nathan Hoffman
& Cruz Williamson.

Two college roommates who decided Tulsa deserved a better detailing company.

Nathan and Cruz were college roommates and friends long before they were business partners. Late nights, big ideas, and one question that kept coming up: what are we actually going to build?

Nathan had been detailing cars since high school. Cruz had worked at a car wash. They came at it from different angles, but they both saw the same gap in the market, one nobody was taking seriously. So they decided to fill it.

They didn’t set out to start a mobile detailing company. They set out to fix an industry, because here is what they kept running into:

  1. Sketchy vans pulling up to detail $80,000 vehicles.
  2. Weak branding that never earned your trust.
  3. Customer service that felt like an afterthought.
  4. Four-hour time windows, and sometimes no one showing up at all.

The home services industry had a reputation problem, and nobody was fixing it.

So they did.

Revived was built to change what people expect when a service shows up at their door: a white-glove experience, professional technicians, and a company that genuinely cares about your car and your time. Nathan and Cruz aren’t just running a detailing business. They’re setting a new standard for mobile service in Tulsa, and eventually, across the nation.
