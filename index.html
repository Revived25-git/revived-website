FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before booking your first detail.

What is Revived Detail?

Revived is a mobile auto detailing business based in Tulsa, Oklahoma. We come to your home, apartment, or office to provide professional interior and exterior detailing — no shop visit required.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the quote form on our Contact page, or call or text us directly at 918-200-9554. Tell us where you are and what your car needs, and we'll follow up with next steps.

Where do you detail the car? Can you come to apartments or parking garages?

We detail wherever your car is parked — driveways, apartment complexes, office lots, and parking garages. If you're in a gated community or garage, just let us know the access details when you book.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Yes — Revived is licensed and insured, and we stand behind every detail we do.

What if I'm not satisfied with the results?

We're human, and accidents happen. If anything doesn't meet your expectations, let us know and we'll come back and fix it — free of charge.

Do I need a service plan, or can I book one-time?

Service plans are optional. You're welcome to book a one-time Interior or Full Detail any time — but Revived Care Plan members save $60–$100 per visit with Quarterly, Bi-Monthly, or Monthly scheduling.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Tulsa and serve the greater Tulsa metro, including Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Owasso, Sand Springs, Sapulpa, Glenpool, Coweta, Catoosa, Claremore, Collinsville, and Skiatook. Not sure if we reach you? Just ask.
