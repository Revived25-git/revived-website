Questions

Gift card answers.

Do gift cards expire?

No. Revived Detail gift cards never expire.

How is the gift card delivered?

Right after checkout we send it to the recipient by email, text or both, depending on the contact info you give us. You get a copy too, so you can forward or print it.

What can a gift card be used for?

Any service or add-on. A gift card is a dollar amount, so it works toward interior detailing, exterior detailing, a full detail, add-ons and more. The amounts shown for services are a guide.

How does the recipient use it?

They book at revivedhq.app/booking, or text or call 918-200-9554, and give us the gift card code. We apply it to their visit. If it doesn't cover the whole service, they pay the difference.

What if they don't use the full amount?

The leftover balance stays on the card for next time.

Can I pick the design?

Yes. Choose our classic Revived Auto Detail Co. card or an occasion design: birthday, Christmas, happy holidays, thank you, anniversary, Father's Day, Mother's Day, congratulations or just because.
