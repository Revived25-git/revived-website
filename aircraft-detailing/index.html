Questions

Aircraft detailing, answered.

What is Revived Aircraft Detailing?

We are the aircraft detailing arm of Revived Detail, the Tulsa mobile detailing company. We come to your hangar, tie-down or ramp to wash, polish, protect and clean aircraft interiors.

Which airports do you serve?

We serve airports within about a two-hour drive of Tulsa, including fields across northeast Oklahoma, central Oklahoma, western Arkansas, southwest Missouri and southeast Kansas. See our airport list, and ask if your field is not listed.

Do you come to my hangar?

Yes. Mobile service is what we do. We work at hangars, tie-downs and FBO ramps where the airport allows it.

Do I need to provide water or power?

Usually not. We use waterless and low-water methods and bring our own supplies. If we need anything special, we will tell you when we schedule.

What kinds of aircraft do you detail?

Single-engine and twin piston aircraft, turboprops and light jets are the core of our work. Tell us about larger aircraft or helicopters and we will let you know what we can take on.

How much does aircraft detailing cost?

Pricing depends on the aircraft type, size, condition and the services you choose. Send us your aircraft and airport and we will give you a clear estimate.
