Airport Service

Aircraft Detailing at
Wiley Post Airport (PWA).

Revived Aircraft Detailing comes to Wiley Post Airport (PWA) in Oklahoma City, OK. Interior cabin detail, exterior wash and degrease, leading edge cleaning and add-ons at your hangar or ramp.

Oklahoma City, OK is about 1 hr 45 min southwest of Tulsa. We schedule PWA details in advance so we can plan the trip, and we can often combine several aircraft at the same field into one visit.

Wiley Post is a busy general aviation airport on the west side of Oklahoma City, popular with corporate and private owners.

Owners and operators based at PWA can have their aircraft washed, degreased, polished, protected and cleaned inside without flying anywhere. We work around your flying and maintenance schedule, and we coordinate access with you.

Aircraft detailing services at PWA

  • Interior Cabin Detail — A deep, restorative clean for leather, upholstery and hard surfaces, hand-finished throughout the cabin and cockpit.
  • Exterior Wash & Degrease — Oil, soot and exhaust streaking lifted without harsh abrasives, for a streak-free finish that protects paint instead of wearing it down.
  • Leading Edge Cleaning — Focused cleaning of the leading edges, where bugs and grime collect first, between your full washes.
  • Ceramic Coating — Ultra-durable protection for exterior surfaces, built to shed grime between visits.
  • Carpet Shampoo — Deep fiber cleaning that lifts odor and embedded dirt from cabin carpet.
  • Leather Conditioner — Nourishment for hide surfaces that helps prevent drying, cracking and premature wear.
  • Odor Elimination — Cabin-safe treatment that neutralizes odor at the source rather than masking it.
  • Brightwork Polish — Hand-polished metal trim and brightwork, brought back to a mirror finish.
  • Window & Windshield Care — Streak-free, haze-free windows cleaned with methods and products made for aircraft acrylic.

Good to know before we come

  • Tell us your aircraft make and model so we can plan time and products.
  • Let your FBO or airport manager know we are coming if required. We are happy to help coordinate.
  • A hangar is ideal, but we can often work outside on the ramp in good weather.
  • We are detailers, not mechanics. We will point out anything unusual we notice.

PWA aircraft detailing questions

Do you detail aircraft at Wiley Post Airport?

Yes. We come to PWA and work at your hangar, tie-down or ramp where the airport allows it.

How far ahead should I book for PWA?

We recommend booking a week or more ahead, so we can fit Oklahoma City into our schedule and coordinate access with you or the airport.

Do I need to provide water or power at PWA?

Usually not. We bring our own supplies and use waterless and low-water methods, so most jobs need no hookups.

How much does aircraft detailing cost at PWA?

Pricing depends on your aircraft type, size, condition and the services you choose. Send us the details and we will give you a clear estimate.
