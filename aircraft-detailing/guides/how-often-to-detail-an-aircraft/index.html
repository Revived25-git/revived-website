How often should you detail an aircraft?

There is no single schedule, but a few simple rules cover most owners.

How often to detail an aircraft depends on where it lives, how much it flies and what you want from it. These are practical starting points for Oklahoma owners.

Hangared aircraft

An aircraft kept in a hangar collects less weather and sun damage. A thorough wash once or twice a year, plus a light wipe-down after dusty or buggy trips, is enough for many owners.

Outdoor or tie-down aircraft

Sun, wind, rain and birds work on the finish every day. Plan on a wash every one to three months, wax or sealant every few months, and a more thorough detail once or twice a year.

Heavy-use and rental aircraft

Aircraft that fly many hours a week pick up bugs, oil and grime quickly. A regular wash schedule keeps them looking professional between deeper details.

Interiors

Plan on an interior detail once or twice a year, and more often if you carry pets, children or clients regularly.

Before you sell

A full detail is one of the cheapest ways to improve how an aircraft presents to buyers. Exterior, interior, windows and brightwork all matter.
