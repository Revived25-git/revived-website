Aircraft Service

Aircraft carpet shampoo in Tulsa, OK.

Deep fiber cleaning that lifts odor and embedded dirt from cabin carpet.

Cabin carpet collects dirt, spills, pet hair and odor that a vacuum cannot reach. A proper deep clean brings back the color and freshens the cabin.

We pre-treat stains, clean the fibers with aircraft-appropriate products and take care not to over-wet the floor.

What is included

  • Vacuum and pre-treatment of stains
  • Deep fiber cleaning of cabin carpet
  • Floor mat cleaning
  • Spot treatment of heavy staining
  • Controlled moisture so carpet dries quickly

Who it is for

  • Owners who carry pets, children or passengers
  • Aircraft with visible stains or odor
  • Owners preparing to sell

Common questions

Can you remove every stain?

Many stains lift completely, but some set permanently. We will tell you what is realistic before we start.

How long until the carpet is dry?

We use low-moisture methods, so drying is usually quick. We will tell you what to expect.

Is this included in an interior cabin detail?

Light carpet cleaning is. Deep shampooing is an add-on.
