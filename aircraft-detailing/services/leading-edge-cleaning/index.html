Aircraft leading edge cleaning in Tulsa, OK.

Focused cleaning of the leading edges, where bugs and grime collect first, between your full washes.

The leading edges of the wings, tail and propeller take the worst of every flight. Bugs, bird residue and grime collect there first, and they are the hardest to remove once they bake on in the sun.

This quick, focused service cleans just the leading edges and nearby surfaces, so you can keep the airframe looking sharp between full exterior washes, or before a trip.

What is included

  • Wing leading edge cleaning
  • Horizontal and vertical stabilizer leading edges
  • Propeller blades and spinner area cleaning
  • Bug and residue removal
  • Aircraft-safe, non-abrasive products
  • Quick walk-around with you

Who it is for

  • Pilots who fly through a lot of bugs
  • Owners who want a fast refresh between full washes
  • Flight schools and rental fleets with tight turnaround

Common questions

Is this the same as a full wash?

No. Leading edge cleaning is a targeted service for the front edges of the wings, tail and prop. For the whole airframe, choose Exterior Wash & Degrease.

How long does it take?

Usually much less than a full wash. We confirm timing when we quote.

Can I add it to a bigger job?

Yes. It pairs well with an interior cabin detail or a full exterior wash.
