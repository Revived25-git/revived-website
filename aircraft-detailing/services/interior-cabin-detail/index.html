Aircraft Service

Aircraft interior cabin detail in Tulsa, OK.

A deep, restorative clean for leather, upholstery and hard surfaces, hand-finished throughout the cabin and cockpit.

The cabin is where you and your passengers spend the flight. Dust, crumbs, sunscreen, pet hair and everyday wear build up quickly, and tight spaces make it hard to reach everything.

We vacuum and clean the whole cabin and cockpit, treat leather and vinyl, clean carpet and wipe down panels and trim with products chosen to be safe for aircraft interiors.

What is included

  • Full cabin, cockpit and baggage area vacuum
  • Seat, leather and vinyl cleaning
  • Carpet and floor mat cleaning
  • Panel, trim and sidewall wipe-down
  • Interior window and instrument glass care
  • Final walk-through with you

Who it is for

  • Owners who fly family, clients or pets
  • Charter and rental operators between customers
  • Anyone selling or listing an aircraft

Common questions

Is it safe around avionics?

Yes. We apply cleaner to the cloth, not the panel, and we avoid saturating switches, screens and instruments.

Do you work inside hangars?

Yes. A hangar is ideal because it keeps dust and weather out while we work.

Can you add leather conditioning or odor removal?

Yes. Both are available as add-ons. Tell us what you would like when you request an estimate.
