Aircraft Service

Aircraft belly, gear & wheel cleaning in Tulsa, OK.

Degreasing and cleaning for the parts of the airplane that collect the most grime.

The underside of an aircraft collects oil, exhaust and road-like grime from every flight. It rarely gets the attention it deserves, yet a dirty belly and gear are easy to spot on a walk-around.

We degrease and clean the belly, landing gear, wheel pants and wheels so the whole aircraft looks cared for, and so oil leaks or other issues are easier to spot.

What is included

  • Belly degreasing and wash
  • Landing gear leg and strut cleaning
  • Wheel and wheel pant cleaning
  • Brake dust removal
  • Light protective finish where appropriate
  • Notes on anything we spot that looks unusual

Who it is for

  • Owners who want a complete top-to-bottom clean
  • Aircraft with heavy oil or exhaust staining
  • Pre-purchase or pre-inspection prep

Common questions

Is this an inspection?

No. We are not a maintenance shop. A clean underside can make issues easier to see, and we will tell you what we notice, but inspections belong with your mechanic.

Do you remove tires or wheels?

No. We clean in place, and we leave mechanical work to your maintenance provider.

Can you do this on its own?

Yes, or as part of a full exterior service.
