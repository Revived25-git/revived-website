Pet Hair Removal in Tulsa, OK.

Pet hair is stubborn. We handle it so your car does not look like you lost a fight with a golden retriever.

Pet hair weaves into carpet and fabric, so a regular vacuum often leaves most of it behind. Light pet hair removal is part of every interior detail we do.

If your car has a heavy coat of it, we can quote the extra time needed. Tell us about your pets when you request a quote and we will plan for it.

What is included

  • Light pet hair removal included in every interior detail
  • Air compressor blowout to loosen hair from seams
  • Vacuuming of seats, carpets and cargo area
  • Optional steam and shampoo for fabric

Who it is for

  • Dog and cat owners
  • Cars used for the vet, groomer or dog park
  • Anyone preparing a car for sale

Common questions

Is pet hair removal included?

Light pet hair removal is included in the interior detail. For heavy pet hair, request a quote and mention it.

Will it remove pet odors?

Pairing the interior detail with odor removal and steam cleaning gives the best results.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can reach the vehicle, you do not need to be there.
