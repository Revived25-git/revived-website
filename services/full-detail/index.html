Full Car Detail in Tulsa, OK.

Interior and exterior, done in one visit. Our most popular way to experience revival.

A full detail combines our complete interior detail with the exterior hand wash and finish. It is the fastest way to take a car from tired to like new without taking it anywhere.

Because the exterior is only $50 on top of the interior, most customers choose the full detail. It is also the best starting point if you plan to move onto a care plan afterward.

What is included

  • Everything in the interior detail
  • Everything in the exterior detail
  • Hand wash with foam pre-soak
  • Streak-free windows inside and out
  • Tire dressing and wheel cleaning
  • 1-month spray wax protection

Who it is for

  • First-time customers
  • Cars being sold, traded or returned from a lease
  • Anyone getting ready for an event or road trip
  • Vehicles that have not been detailed in a while

Common questions

What is the difference between interior and full detail?

The interior detail cleans the cabin. The full detail adds the exterior hand wash and finish. See our guide on interior versus full detail for more.

Can I add extras?

Yes. Steam and shampoo, odor removal, engine detail, protection and ceramic coating can all be added.

Can I put this on a plan?

Yes. Revived Care Plans save $60 to $100 off every service.
