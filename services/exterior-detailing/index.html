Exterior Car Detailing in Tulsa, OK.

A hand wash and finish that brings the outside back to life, right where your car is parked.

Oklahoma is hard on paint: red dust, pollen, summer bugs and baked-on road grime. Our exterior detail is a hand wash, not a drive-through, so we can reach the places an automatic wash misses.

The exterior is an add-on to any interior detail for $50, which makes it an easy way to get the whole car done in one visit.

What is included

  • Hand wash and foam pre-soak
  • Wheels, tires and wheel wells deep cleaned
  • Tire dressing applied
  • Bugs and road grime removed
  • Door jambs and trim cleaned
  • Streak-free glass, towel dried
  • 1-month spray wax protection

Who it is for

  • Anyone who wants the whole car done in one visit
  • Trucks and SUVs with heavy road buildup
  • Cars that sit outside under the Oklahoma sun

Common questions

How much is the exterior detail?

The exterior is $50 when added to an interior detail.

Is this a hand wash?

Yes. It is a hand wash with a foam pre-soak, not an automatic or brush wash.

Can you protect the paint longer?

Yes. Our Revived’s Way protection and 1-year ceramic coating add-ons are available. Ask for a quote.
