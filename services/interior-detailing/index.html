Interior Car Detailing in Tulsa, OK.

A full interior reset for your car, SUV or truck, done in your driveway or office lot.

Your interior is where you spend your time, and it collects everything: crumbs, dust, coffee rings, pet hair and the grime that builds up on every touch point. Our interior detail works through the cabin zone by zone, so nothing gets skipped and nothing gets rushed.

We bring our own water and power, so there is nothing to set up. You stay home, at work, or wherever your day takes you, and we handle the rest.

What is included

  • Trash removal
  • Air compressor blowout of vents, seams and tight spots
  • Vacuum of every nook and cranny
  • Console, dash and cup holders disinfected
  • Seats wiped down
  • Floor mats scrubbed and cleaned
  • Doors cleaned, streak-free windows
  • Light stain and pet hair removal

Who it is for

  • Daily drivers and commuters
  • Parents with kids and car seats
  • Pet owners
  • Anyone selling or trading in a vehicle
  • Rideshare and sales drivers

Common questions

How long does an interior detail take?

It depends on the vehicle and how much needs to be done. We will give you a clear expectation when you book, and we arrive within a set window, not a four-hour block.

Do you need access to water or power?

No. We bring our own water and power, so you do not need to provide a hookup.

Can I add steam cleaning or odor removal?

Yes. Seat, carpet and headliner shampoo and steam, odor removal and other add-ons can be added to any interior detail.
